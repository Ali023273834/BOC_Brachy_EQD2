Constraints and α/β

Designed by Medical Physicist Ali Eid Supervised by Dr. Zainab Alhaddad Tested by Medical Physicist Hamad Almannai and the Radiation Oncology team
BED = n·d·(1 + d / (α/β))  ·  EQD2 = BED / (1 + 2 / (α/β)) Linear-quadratic model with complete repair between fractions; no correction for overall treatment time. EBRT phases are summed separately. The de-escalation estimate assumes the next plan uses the same applicator geometry so organ doses scale in proportion to the prescription; re-optimising the dwell pattern can do better than simple scaling. Constraint presets follow EMBRACE II / GEC-ESTRO for intrauterine and interstitial cervix brachytherapy and are editable. Confirm all values against the departmental protocol.
